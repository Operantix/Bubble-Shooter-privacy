Summary: Operantix respects your privacy. Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix does not collect or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number. We utilize trusted third-party partners (such as Google AdMob and Google Play Services) to provide advertisements, in-app billing, and ensure game stability.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

When you play Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix, our game and integrated third-party SDKs may automatically collect certain device and interaction metrics to support core gameplay, including:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version (Android OS), screen resolution, language settings, and network connection type.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) used solely for frequency capping, ad attribution, and non-personalized / personalized ad delivery.
  • Game Progress Data: Levels cleared, high scores, collected stars, and purchased boosters (stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences / LocalStorage).
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Crash logs, error traces, and runtime performance indicators to fix technical bugs.

2. Third-Party Services & Technologies

Our application integrates third-party services that collect data governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review their terms:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform connectivity and application updates.
    https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads SDK): Displaying rewarded video ads, interstitial banners, and lucky spin reward advertisements.
    Google AdMob Privacy Overview
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Secure payment processing for removing advertisements and purchasing virtual coin packs. Operantix never sees or stores your credit card or financial credentials.
    Google Play Terms of Service
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Facilitating consent gathering compliant with GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and ePrivacy directives in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use the data collected strictly for legitimate game operations:

  • To provide and maintain gameplay mechanics and level progression.
  • To grant rewards when you watch optional rewarded video ads (such as free spins on the Lucky Wheel or +5 revive bubbles).
  • To prevent fraud, verify ad impressions, and troubleshoot technical errors.
  • To fulfill and maintain purchases (such as permanently removing advertisements).

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix is designed as a general-audience casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we discover that personal information has been collected from a child without verified parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete that information from our records.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your game progress, stars, and coins are stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You may reset or permanently delete all local game data at any time by navigating to:

Android Settings > Apps > Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Cache, or by uninstalling the app.

For data collected by Google Play or AdMob, you can manage or reset your Google Advertising ID in your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.

6. Security of Your Information

We prioritize the security of all communications. All data exchanged between the app and third-party servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

Your continued use of the application following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of those updates.
