Privacy Policy for Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix

1 Introduction

Welcome to Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix ("App", "Game", "we", "us", or "our"), published by Operantix. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data collected when you interact with our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and protected when you download, install, and play Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix through the Google Play Store, in strict accordance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2 Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The game is designed so that you can play without creating an account or providing sensitive personal information like your real name, email, physical address, or phone number.

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game State & Progression: Level index, stars earned per level, high scores, total points, and unlocked levels.
  • Booster Inventory: Remaining counts for Bomb, Rainbow, and Laser boosters.
  • Audio & Interface Settings: Sound FX on/off preferences.
  • Ad-Free Status: Whether the "Remove Ads" permanent in-app purchase has been unlocked.

Note: This data is stored strictly locally in your device's sandbox storage via Android SharedPreferences/HTML5 LocalStorage and is not transmitted to any private Operantix servers.

B. Automatically Collected Technical & Advertising Data

When you play with an active Internet connection, third-party libraries integrated into the app (such as Google Mobile Ads SDK / AdMob) may collect certain technical identifiers to serve advertisements, prevent fraud, and maintain service stability:

Data Category Examples Purpose
Device Identifiers Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID Ad personalization (with user consent), ad frequency capping, fraud detection.
Technical & Hardware Specs Device model, manufacturer, OS version, screen resolution, locale Rendering optimization, high-FPS canvas adaptation, crash analytics.
Network & Location IP address, general connection type (Wi-Fi/Cellular) Determining broad/approximate geographic region for relevant regional advertisements.
Diagnostics & Performance Crash traces, ANR diagnostics, frame jank metrics Monitoring game stability and performance improvements.

3 In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix offers an optional digital in-app purchase to remove all banner and interstitial advertisements permanently across your Google account:

  • Product: "Remove Ads" (remove_ads_lifetime)
  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions and payment data (such as credit cards, UPI, PayPal, or bank details) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing on Google’s secure servers. Operantix never collects, views, or stores your credit card or financial information.
  • Purchase Tokens: Google Play sends our app an encrypted purchase token verifying that the purchase succeeded. Our game acknowledges this token via the official Google Play Billing Library to permanently silence advertisements and restores this entitlement if you reinstall the app.

5 GDPR & European Economic Area (EEA) / UK Privacy Rights

If you reside within the European Union, EEA, or United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Consent Mechanism: On your first launch, the Google UMP consent banner will request your explicit permission before loading personalized ads.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw or adjust your consent preferences at any time by resetting your ad identifiers in your Android system settings.
  • Right to Erasure & Access: Because we do not maintain accounts or back-end databases of personal records, your gameplay records exist solely on your physical device. Uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Data" in Android App Settings instantly erases all stored local progression and identifiers.

6 California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act, California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected and request that it not be sold or shared:

  • We do not sell your personal information for monetary value.
  • Third-party advertisers (Google AdMob) may utilize identifiers to deliver cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out at the operating system level:
    Android Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

7 Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Bubble Shooter Pop Operantix is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

Notice to Parents and Guardians

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information or if you have questions regarding child privacy, please contact us immediately at our support email below and we will take prompt steps to investigate and remove any associated records.

All communication between our application, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing is strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 protocols. Local game records are sandboxed within the application’s private Android storage space, inaccessible to other apps.
